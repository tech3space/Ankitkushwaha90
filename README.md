## 📖 Foundational Transformer Research I Study & Implement

I actively read, implement, and extend key papers that shaped modern AI:

1. **Attention Is All You Need (2017)** — Vaswani et al.  
   *Foundation of Transformers. Introduced self-attention and parallelism.*

2. **BERT (2018)** — Devlin et al.  
   Bidirectional pre-training for deep language understanding.

3. **GPT (2018)** — Radford et al.  
   Generative pre-training and decoder-only revolution.

4. **GPT-3 (2020)** — Brown et al.  
   Scaling laws and few-shot learning.

5. **Transformer-XL (2019)** — Long-range dependencies beyond fixed context.

6. **ViT (2020)** — Vision Transformers: Images as sequences.

7. **T5 (2020)** — Unified text-to-text framework.

8. **FlashAttention (2022)** — Dao et al.  
   Fast and memory-efficient exact attention.

**Recommended Reading Order:**  
Attention Is All You Need → BERT → GPT → GPT-3 → FlashAttention
